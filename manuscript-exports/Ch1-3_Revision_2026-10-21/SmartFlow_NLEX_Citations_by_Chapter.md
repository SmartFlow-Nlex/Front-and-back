# SmartFlow NLEX – In-Text Citations and References by Chapter

*Generated 22 October 2026 by comparing the original manuscript with the revised manuscript.*

## How the sources were verified

The citation agents checked each entry against an authoritative record:

- **Crossref:** the DOI registry, which confirms authors, title, journal, volume, issue, pages and year.
- **Publisher pages and arXiv.**
- **OpenAlex, PubMed and Semantic Scholar:** these supplied abstracts, which were used to check whether a source actually supports the claim it is cited for.
- **Official websites:** IPCC, FHWA, the National Academies, MPTC, W3C, Nielsen Norman Group and the Inquirer (through the Wayback Machine).

Each source has one of four verification levels:

- **V-full:** metadata confirmed, and the claim was checked against the abstract or full text.
- **V-meta:** metadata confirmed; the claim was checked against the title or scope only. It is used here only for what the title states.
- **V-web:** an official web page was read directly.
- **Check:** real, but some reference details are unconfirmed (listed in Citation Audit §4).

No reference in the revised manuscript was created without one of these checks. The full metadata evidence is in the agents' files (`findings/09a–09d`).

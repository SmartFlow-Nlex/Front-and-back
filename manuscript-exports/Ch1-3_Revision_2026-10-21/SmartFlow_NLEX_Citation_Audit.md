## 5. Update, 24 October 2026
Eighteen verified references were added to support methods the system implements (gradient boosting, GWR, ordinal and logistic incident models, bootstrap, toll-booth staffing, Takagi–Sugeno inference and two-stage stochastic programming). The table is in Citations_by_Chapter.md. The automated cross-check was re-run on the rebuilt manuscript: 122 entries, every entry cited, and every citation matched to an entry. Notes for the team:
- Li et al. (2022) and Wen et al. (2021) are in MDPI journals (indexed, not predatory). Replace them if the panel objects to MDPI.
- Birge & Louveaux (2011): Crossref does not record the edition. "2nd ed." is confirmed by the author's page and library records.
- Efron & Tibshirani (1993): the imprint is Chapman & Hall, while Crossref lists Springer US as the digital host.
- Takagi & Sugeno (1985) presents linear rule outputs. The manuscript describes the zero-order (constant-output) form as a special case.
- No peer-reviewed two-stage model for freeway incident dispatch was found. Rawls & Turnquist (2010) is the closest emergency-response analogue, and the text presents it as such.

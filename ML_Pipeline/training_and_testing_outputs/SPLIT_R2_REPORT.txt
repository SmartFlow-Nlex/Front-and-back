  SPLIT R² ANALYSIS — TRAINING vs VALIDATION
  (Adviser-Recommended Overfitting Diagnostic)
================================================================================

  Methodology:
    - For each model, we compute R² on TRAINING data and VALIDATION data separately
    - This is done for each of the 3 walk-forward folds
    - The GAP (Train R² - Val R²) reveals overfitting behavior

  Interpretation Key:
    Train R² HIGH + Val R² HIGH + Gap SMALL  → JUST RIGHT
    Train R² HIGH + Val R² LOW  + Gap LARGE  → OVERFITTING
    Train R² LOW  + Val R² LOW               → UNDERFITTING
